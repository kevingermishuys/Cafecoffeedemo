Café Coffee Windhoek is part of the EMONEKO Group, run from the EMONEKO Building in Olympia — the corner where a lot of Windhoek starts its day.

Windhoek's favourite corner

Come as you are

Whether you're catching up with friends or carving out a quiet moment for yourself, Café Coffee Windhoek is the go-to spot. Grab a coffee and a slice of something good, settle in with free WiFi, or swing by between meetings — the parking is easy and the coffee is always fresh.

Between the Brötchen, the daily pie and whatever's new on the chalkboard, the menu keeps a local rhythm: familiar enough to be a habit, changing enough to be worth the visit.
